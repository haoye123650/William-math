Section A Multiple Choice

20 marks

1.

(2 marks)

Notes: (−2)³ = −8, so −(−8) = 8. Then 8 + 4 = 12.

(2 marks)

Section C Conventional Questions

30 marks

6 marks each. Total 30 marks. Show your working.

21.
Find the value of (−1)4 − (−1)3 × (−2)2.
(6 marks)

Answer: 5

Notes: 1 − (−1) × 4 = 1 − (−4) = 5.

Marking: Powers 2 marks; order of operations 2 marks; 5 a further 2 marks.

22.
Find the value of (34 − 23) ÷ 112 − 12.
(6 marks)

Answer: 1/2

Notes: 34 − 23 = 112. Then 1 − 12 = 12.

Marking: Bracket 2 marks; division 2 marks; final 2 marks. 0.5 accepted.

23.
Solve x + 14 − 2x − 16 = 1.
(6 marks)

Answer: x = −7

Notes: ×12: 3(x + 1) − 2(2x − 1) = 12
3x + 3 − 4x + 2 = 12
−x + 5 = 12
x = −7

Marking: Clearing denominators 2 marks; expanding 2 marks; x = −7 a further 2 marks.

24.

(6 marks)

Answer: x = 6

Notes: 6x − 15 + 4 = 5x − 5
6x − 11 = 5x − 5
x = 6

Section D Structured Questions

30 marks

10 marks each. Total 30 marks. Set up equations or expressions. Include units.

26.
A phone is priced at $4800. It is first sold at 15% off, then a further 8% off the discounted price.
(a) Find the final selling price.
(b) Find the overall percentage discount on the original price.
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) $3753.60; (b) 21.8%

Notes: (a) 4800 × 0.85 = 4080; 4080 × 0.92 = 3753.6.
(b) 1 − 3753.6/4800 = 0.218 = 21.8%.

Marking: (a) 6 marks (allow 3753.6); (b) 4 marks.

27.
Towns A and B are 360 km apart. Car A travels at 80 km/h and car B at 40 km/h towards each other. Car B starts 1 hour later than car A.
(a) How long after car A sets off do they meet?
(b) How far has car A travelled when they meet?
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) 10/3 h (3 h 20 min); (b) 800/3 km (about 266.7 km)

Notes: After 1 h, car A has gone 80 km; 280 km remain. Time then = 280/120 = 7/3 h.
Total time = 1 + 7/3 = 10/3 h. Distance = 80 × 10/3 = 800/3 km.

Marking: (a) 6 marks; (b) 4 marks. Mixed numbers or decimals accepted if exact enough.

28.
AB // CD. A transversal meets AB at E and CD at F. ∠AEF = 58°.
(a) Find the corresponding angle to ∠AEF.
(b) Find the alternate angle to ∠AEF.
(c) Find the co-interior angle to ∠AEF.
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) 58°; (b) 58°; (c) 122°

Notes: Corresponding and alternate angles are equal to 58°. Co-interior: 180° − 58° = 122°.

Marking: 3 marks, 3 marks, 4 marks.

29.
The sum of three consecutive integers is 96. Find the three integers.
(5 marks)

Answer: 31, 32, 33

Notes: n + (n+1) + (n+2) = 96, 3n = 93, n = 31.

Marking: Equation 2 marks; three integers 3 marks.

30.
A map has scale 1 : 50 000. The distance between two towns on the map is 4 cm. Find the actual distance in km.
(5 marks)

Answer: 2 km

Notes: 4 × 50 000 = 200 000 cm = 2000 m = 2 km.

Marking: Scale product 2 marks; convert to km 3 marks.

How to read the score

  • 90–100Strong S.1. Ready for S.2 factorization and simultaneous equations.
  • 75–89Solid. Drill fractional equations and successive percentages before S.2.
  • 60–74Return to Test 1 gaps: directed numbers, transposing, and order of operations.
  • below 60Do Test 1 again first. Do not start S.2 yet.

When marking, watch

  • Q1 and Q21 nest powers inside a leading minus. If both fail, reteach (−a)ⁿ.
  • Q7 is the classic +25% then −20% trap; net change is not +5%.
  • Q23 has a negative unknown. Watch the sign when isolating x.
  • Q26: the second discount is taken from the reduced price.
  • Q27: B starts one hour later. Subtract the head-start distance first.
