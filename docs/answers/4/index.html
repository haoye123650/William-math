Section C Conventional Questions

30 marks

6 marks each. Total 30 marks. Show your working.

21.
Factorize 2x2 + 5x − 3.
(6 marks)

Answer: (2x − 1)(x + 3)

Notes: 2x × x = 2x², −1 × 3 = −3, middle term 6x − x = 5x.

Marking: Correct pair 4 marks; written as a product 2 marks.

22.
In a 30°–60°–90° triangle, the side opposite 30° is 5 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
(6 marks)

Answer: 10 cm

Notes: The hypotenuse is twice the side opposite 30°.

Marking: Ratio stated 3 marks; 10 cm 3 marks.

23.
Solve the simultaneous equations
x + 2y = 8
3x − y = 3
(6 marks)

Answer: x = 2, y = 3

Notes: y = 3x − 3. Then x + 2(3x − 3) = 8 → 7x = 14 → x = 2, y = 3.

Marking: One unknown 3 marks; the other 3 marks.

24.
Solve x2 − 4x − 5 = 0 by factorization.
(6 marks)

Answer: x = 5 or x = −1

Notes: (x − 5)(x + 1) = 0.

Marking: Factors 3 marks; both roots 3 marks.

25.
Two similar triangles have corresponding sides 6 cm and 9 cm. The smaller has area 20 cm2. Find the area of the larger triangle.
(6 marks)

Answer: 45 cm²

Notes: Length ratio 2 : 3, area ratio 4 : 9. Larger = 20 × 9/4 = 45.

Marking: Area ratio 3 marks; 45 cm² 3 marks.

Section D Structured Questions

30 marks

10 marks each. Total 30 marks. Set up equations. Include units.

26.
A jacket is priced at $400. It is first sold at 20% off, then a further 10% off the discounted price.
(a) Find the final selling price.
(b) Find the overall percentage discount on the original price.
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) $288; (b) 28%

Notes: 400 × 0.8 × 0.9 = 288. Discount = (400 − 288)/400 = 28%.

Marking: (a) 6 marks; (b) 4 marks.

27.
A sector of a circle of radius 7 cm has angle 90°.
(a) Find the arc length in terms of π.
(b) Find the area of the sector in terms of π.
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) 3.5π cm; (b) 12.25π cm²

Notes: Arc = (90/360)×2π×7 = 7π/2. Area = (90/360)π×49 = 49π/4.

Marking: (a) 5 marks; (b) 5 marks. 7π/2 or 3.5π both accepted.

28.
A(0, 0), B(6, 0) and C(6, 8) are the vertices of a triangle.
(a) Find AC.
(b) Explain why ∠ABC = 90°.
(c) Find the area of △ABC.
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) 10; (b) AB is horizontal and BC is vertical; (c) 24

Notes: AC = √(36 + 64) = 10. Area = (1/2)×6×8 = 24.

Marking: (a) 3 marks; (b) 3 marks; (c) 4 marks.

29.
Solve x2 = 2x + 15 by factorization.
(5 marks)

Answer: x = 5 or x = −3

Notes: x² − 2x − 15 = 0, (x − 5)(x + 3) = 0.

Marking: Rearrange 2 marks; both roots 3 marks.

30.
Find the value of sin2 30° + cos2 30°.
(5 marks)

Answer: 1

Notes: sin² θ + cos² θ = 1 for any θ. Directly: (1/2)² + (√3/2)² = 1.

Marking: Full marks for 1, with identity or substitution.

How to read the score

  • 90–100: S.3 is secure. Next: quadratic formula, remainder theorem and the sine rule.
  • 75–89: Keep the methods. Drill special-angle trig and similar-triangle area ratios.
  • 60–74: S.2 factorization is not automatic yet. Revisit (x + p)(x + q) before trig.
  • below 60: Do Tests 2 and 3 first. S.3 will pile up if expanding and Pythagoras are still slow.

When marking, watch

  • Q2, Q12 and Q22 are the special-angle set. If those fail, make a 30-60-90 card this week.
  • Q3 and Q25: length ratio k means area ratio k². That is the usual DSE trap.
  • Q21: 2x² + 5x − 3. Guess (2x − 1)(x + 3), then check the middle term.
  • Q26 is successive percentage change. The second cut is off the discounted price.
  • Q27: sector fraction is θ/360. Do not use the radian formula.
