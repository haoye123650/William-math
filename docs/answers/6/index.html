Section C Conventional Questions

30 marks

6 marks each. Total 30 marks. Show your working.

21.
Solve 2x2 − 5x − 3 = 0 by the quadratic formula.
(6 marks)

Answer: x = 3 or x = −1/2

Notes: Δ = 25 + 24 = 49. x = (5 ± 7)/4 = 3 or −1/2.

Marking: Discriminant 2 marks; both roots 4 marks.

22.
Rewrite x2 + y2 − 6x − 8y = 0 in the form (x − h)2 + (y − k)2 = r2. Hence state the centre and the radius.
(6 marks)

Answer: (x − 3)² + (y − 4)² = 25; centre (3, 4), radius 5

Notes: (x − 3)² − 9 + (y − 4)² − 16 = 0.

Marking: Completing the square 4 marks; centre and radius 2 marks.

23.
A geometric sequence has first term 5 and common ratio 1/2. Find n such that T<sub>n</sub> = 5/16.
(6 marks)

Answer: n = 5

Notes: 5 × (1/2)n−1 = 5/16, (1/2)n−1 = 1/16, n − 1 = 4, n = 5.

Marking: Set up 3 marks; n = 5, 3 marks.

24.
A triangle has sides 5 cm, 5 cm and 6 cm. Find its area.
(6 marks)

Answer: 12 cm²

Notes: Heron: s = 8, √(8×3×3×2) = √144 = 12. Or height to base 6: 4² + 3² = 5², area = (1/2)×6×4 = 12.

Marking: Method 3 marks; 12 cm² 3 marks.

25.
Solve 2x2 + x − 6 ≥ 0.
(6 marks)

Answer: x ≤ −2 or x ≥ 3/2

Notes: (2x − 3)(x + 2) ≥ 0. Critical values −2 and 3/2. Outside the roots because the parabola opens up.

Marking: Factors 3 marks; union of rays 3 marks.

Section D Structured Questions

30 marks

10 marks each. Total 30 marks. Set up equations. Include units.

26.
The line y = x + 1 intersects the circle x2 + y2 = 13 at two points.
(a) Find the coordinates of the two points.
(b) Find the distance between them.
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) (2, 3) and (−3, −2); (b) 5√2

Notes: x² + (x + 1)² = 13 → x² + x − 6 = 0 → (x − 2)(x + 3) = 0. Distance √(5² + 5²) = 5√2.

Marking: (a) 6 marks; (b) 4 marks.

27.
A theatre has 10 rows. The first row has 20 seats. Each row after that has 4 more seats than the row in front.
(a) How many seats are in the 10th row?
(b) How many seats are there in total?
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) 56; (b) 380

Notes: T10 = 20 + 9×4 = 56. S10 = 10/2 × (20 + 56) = 380.

Marking: (a) 4 marks; (b) 6 marks.

28.
A cuboid measures 3 cm by 4 cm by 12 cm.
(a) Find the length of a face diagonal of the 3 cm by 4 cm face.
(b) Find the length of a space diagonal of the cuboid.
(10 marks)

Answer: (a) 5 cm; (b) 13 cm

Notes: Face: √(9+16)=5. Space: √(9+16+144)=√169=13.

Marking: (a) 4 marks; (b) 6 marks.

29.
Let f(x) = 2x − 3 and g(x) = x2. Solve (g ∘ f)(x) = 25.
(5 marks)

Answer: x = 4 or x = −1

Notes: (2x − 3)² = 25, 2x − 3 = ±5, x = 4 or x = −1.

30.
When x3 + ax2 − x + 2 is divided by x − 1, the remainder is 6. Find a.
(5 marks)

Answer: a = 4

Notes: f(1) = 1 + a − 1 + 2 = 6, a + 2 = 6, a = 4.

Marking: Remainder theorem 3 marks; a = 4, 2 marks.

How to read the score

  • 90–100: Paper 1 Section A is in reach. Next: linear programming, more about trigonometry, and past Paper 1 Section A2.
  • 75–89: Keep the algebra. Completing the square and rewriting circle equations need speed.
  • 60–74: S.4 quadratics are not automatic. Revisit the formula and remainder theorem, then retry circles.
  • below 60: Do Tests 4 and 5 first. DSE Paper 1 will pile up if factorization and indices are still slow.

When marking, watch

  • Q3 and Q22: complete the square on x² + y² + Dx + Ey + F = 0. Radius squared is (D/2)² + (E/2)² − F.
  • Q2: infinite GP, |r| &lt; 1, S = a/(1 − r).
  • Q21: Δ first. If they skip the formula and only factor, still award method if the roots are correct.
  • Q26 is line + circle. Substitute y from the line into the circle, then factor the quadratic.
  • Q28 is a 3-4-12 cuboid. Face 3-4-5, space diagonal 13. Same trick as 5-12-13.
